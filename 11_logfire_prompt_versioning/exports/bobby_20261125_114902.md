Bobby, here's how to get started learning about trains and how to fix them:

- Take a beginner course on locomotive or diesel/electric basics online or at a trade school, and review with short daily flashcards.
- Volunteer at a heritage railway or rail yard to get hands-on experience while you study.
- Look for rail apprenticeships or entry-level roles like railcar technician trainee or locomotive helper through unions and railroads.
- Build skills like reading schematics, basic welding, using diagnostic tools, and following safety rules—ask employers which certifications they require for beginners.
